Full hashes are in [`SHA256SUMS`](SHA256SUMS). Checkpoint names are zero-indexed.
The curriculum level is `min(8, counter // 4000)`: one rung per 4,000
per-environment control steps (24 steps per PPO iteration), so the ladder
reaches level 8 (0.24 m) at about iteration 1,333 and the rest of the run
trains the whole [0.08, 0.24] range. The checkpoints at 1,550 and later are
all at level 8; the early files (150-900) are at the lower rungs.

## Measured notes (local replay) on native CPU

These are experiment checkpoints. Replayed at a **fixed** exposed step, the
run does not complete a clean mount on the 0.20-0.24 m rungs: it hooks the
rim and leans, and at 0.24 it also falls. The useful range is the low one.

- `model_3000.pt` at **0.08 m** is the best measured: the root gets onto the
  pedestal footprint (body at the rim, feet on the platform) for long
  stretches; still not the inner-half jackpot.
- `model_5000.pt` at **0.10 m** is the next best.
- `model_9800.pt` at **0.18 m** (and at 0.22 m) leans without a mount.
- At 0.24 m the mechanism cannot make the last step; the run spent its tail
  there, which is why the later checkpoints are not better at the low rungs.

The local notes are `native`/CPU; the official replay is `warp`/GPU and may
differ slightly. Treat the checkpoints as a sweep and pick the one that
works for the height you are testing.
