# Lumen Reef — selected concept

**Robot:** Jumper (22-DoF crab), platform `jumper`.
**Selection:** candidate 1 of 3 presented on 2026-10-17.
**User confirmation:** "1, per la palette in caso la mettiamo dopo se cosi è basilare"

## Intent

A low-profile bioluminescent carapace for a climbing/traversal entry. The shell keeps
the original six-hole interface and the original lower silhouette; only the upper
surface changes.

## Silhouette

- Low, smooth carapace; no tall spines.
- Three dorsal ridges running front-to-back, tapering at both ends.
- Short swept-back rear fin.
- Clearance stays close to the original shell so narrow ledges remain passable.

## Palette (deferred)

Base decision: pearl/ivory shell, deep teal/indigo limbs, cyan joint accents,
magenta claw tips. Final palette to be chosen after the geometry blockout.

## Map pairing

Lumen Rift: dark canyon, stepped basalt ledges, glowing cyan pools, magenta alien
ferns, narrow rock bridge, high ridge finish.

## Boundaries

- Display-only `.skin`; no print delivery.
- Collision and inertia remain baseline (`physical_dynamics_validated=false`).
- No Avatar names, creatures, logos or music; original bioluminescent world only.
